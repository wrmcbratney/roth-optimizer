Model limitations & compliance status
  • Strategy search is heuristic; results are the best modeled strategy under the tested targets and assumptions, not a mathematically proven optimum.
  • Future-year brackets, deductions and IRMAA tiers/surcharges are approximated by indexing the current-year tables at your inflation input (top IRMAA tier held frozen until 2028 per statute); actual figures are set annually by the IRS and CMS.
  • Results are sensitive to return assumptions. Taxable drag is approximated through annual taxation of qualified dividends and does not model embedded-gain liquidation — results may overstate after-tax wealth where liquidation would realize gains.
  • State tax is a rough flat haircut on federal taxable income — it does not reflect state-specific treatment of Social Security, pensions, IRA distributions or capital gains.
  • Preserve-RMD strategies are anchored to the first RMD year; later RMDs drift above the chosen bracket as the IRS life-expectancy divisor shrinks.
  • Taxes are paid from taxable assets; shortfalls beyond the conversion disqualify a strategy unless forced draws are explicitly allowed, and forced draws are modeled without gross-up.
  • Survivor modeling is simplified: no qualifying-surviving-spouse filing status, survivor-benefit claiming rules, IRMAA life-changing-event appeals, remarriage, QCDs, or itemized/charitable strategies.
  • Death-year RMD handling assumes any required final-year RMD was satisfied before the survivor transition. Additional beneficiary-RMD timing is not modeled in client-facing output.
  • This is a planning projection, not a tax return calculation. Conversions require CPA review before execution.
